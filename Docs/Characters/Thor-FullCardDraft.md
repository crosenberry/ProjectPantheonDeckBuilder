# Thor — Full Card Draft (75 cards)

Second deep pass, after [Artemis](Artemis-CardDraft.md). Supersedes the earlier [Thor-CardSketch.md](Thor-CardSketch.md) (kept for history) — every card from the sketch is carried forward here, expanded to full depth. Purpose per the [cross-Blessing recommendation](CrossBlessing-Comparison.md): confirm "similar resource shape, different timing" (Storm vs. Artemis's Volley) actually plays different at full 75-card scale, not just in a 6-card sample.

Combat vocabulary used throughout below (Block, Strength, Exposed, Drained, Sundered, Exhaust, Curse, Energy, draw rules) is now formalized in [GameDesignDocument.md §10](../GameDesignDocument.md).

Recap: **Storm** persists across turns. Block-generating cards grant it as a side effect. Payoff cards discharge banked Storm for burst damage or other effects. Three archetypes established in the sketch, kept as the organizing spine here: **Bulwark** (turtle, then release), **Chain** (small discharge every turn, tempo), **Unrelenting** (Storm-light, HP-for-power berserker). A **Flex** tag marks cards any Thor build reasonably wants regardless of archetype.

## Sanity Checks

**Count**: 3 Basic + 28 Common + 30 Uncommon + 14 Rare = 75. ✓

**Archetype distribution**: Bulwark 20 cards (8C/8U/4R), Chain 18 (7C/7U/4R), Unrelenting 17 (7C/7U/3R), Flex 20 (6C/8U/3R + 3 Basic). Reasonably even — no archetype is starved, and Flex being the largest single bucket is intentional so any Thor deck has enough glue cards regardless of which of the 3 archetypes it leans into.

**Attack/Skill/Power split**: 31 Attack / 28 Skill / 16 Power ≈ 41% / 37% / 21%. Close to Slay the Spire's typical per-character ratio — no rebalancing needed on this axis. Notably zero Powers at Common tier, matching the StS convention of Powers skewing toward Uncommon/Rare.

**Findings from going to full depth**:
- The "similar shape, different timing" hypothesis holds up better at full scale than the 6-card sketch could show: Bulwark cards (Titan's Bulwark, Ragnarok's Herald, Valkyrie's Ward) are all high-cost, high-Block-plus-Storm dumps that reward *not* attacking for a turn — a rhythm Artemis's kit never produces, since Volley punishes waiting. That contrast reads clearly now.
- Chain and Bulwark both got a Power that turns Block generation or Storm consumption into a repeating trigger (Stonebound Stance / Live Current, Overcharge). These need a close look together during tuning — stacking more than one on the same turn could get out of hand, standard multi-Power-synergy risk, same category as flagged elsewhere, not new.
- Deathless Rage (Rare, Unrelenting) is a "safety net" effect — confirmed to stay a card, not become a relic (see GDD §10): being Rare *and* needing to be drawn already gates it well, whereas a relic version would be an always-on insurance policy with no real opportunity cost once picked up. Retooled to key off damage taken this combat (matching Last Stand's existing pattern in this same archetype) rather than a flat Strength number — keeps it archetype-consistent with Unrelenting's "took a beating, hit back harder" identity, and reads as Thor earning his defiance through endurance rather than a generic resurrection effect borrowed from elsewhere. The same beat shows up independently in Anubis's and Sun Wukong's Rare tiers too (Osiris's Rebirth, Elixir of Immortality) — not a coincidence given how common death/rebirth themes are across mythology in general, and each Blessing expresses it through its own mechanic (Thor: damage taken; Anubis: Scale; Wukong: Form), so it doesn't read as the same card copy-pasted three times.
- ~~Two near-duplicate "look at top 3, discard any" Skills exist (Norn's Insight at Common, Ravens' Sight at Uncommon)~~ — **Fixed in the M5 design session** ([../ImplementationRoadmap.md](../ImplementationRoadmap.md)): Ravens' Sight now also grants 1 Storm, differentiating it from Norn's Insight and justifying the Uncommon-over-Common rarity bump.

**Not attempted**: any numeric tuning/playtesting, card art/flavor text, exact enemy-facing numbers to validate Block/damage values against. This is still a rule-shape-and-scope draft, same caveat as Artemis's.
