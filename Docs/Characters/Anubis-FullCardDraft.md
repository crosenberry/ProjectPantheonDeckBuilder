# Anubis — Full Card Draft (75 cards)

Third deep pass, after [Artemis](Artemis-CardDraft.md) and [Thor](Thor-FullCardDraft.md). Supersedes the earlier [Anubis-CardSketch.md](Anubis-CardSketch.md) (kept for history) — every card from the sketch is carried forward here, expanded to full depth.

Recap: **Scale** is a signed value (-5 to +5, Order/+ vs. Chaos/-), resets to 0 each combat. Cards push it as a side effect. Payoff cards read its *position* — magnitude, sign, or closeness to 0 — not a simple stack count. Three archetypes established in the sketch, kept as the organizing spine: **Balance-keeper** (hover near 0), **Reaper** (push toward Chaos/-), **Ascendant** (push toward Order/+). A **Flex** tag marks cards any Anubis build reasonably wants.

## Sanity Checks

**Count**: 3 Basic + 28 Common + 30 Uncommon + 14 Rare = 75. ✓

**Archetype distribution**: Balance-keeper 17 (7C/7U/3R), Reaper 19 (7C/8U/4R), Ascendant 19 (7C/8U/4R), Flex 17 (7C/7U/3R + 3 Basic). Even spread, no path starved.

**Attack/Skill/Power split**: 29 Attack / 33 Skill / 13 Power ≈ 39% / 44% / 17%. Skill-heavy relative to Thor's 41/37/21 — reads as intentional given Egyptian's Utility/Sustain focus (more Skills = more utility/sustain tools), but worth double-checking against Artemis and Thor side by side once all four are done, in case it's more skew than the flavor justifies.

**Findings from going to full depth**:
- The bidirectional-Scale "3 paths for free" hypothesis from the light sketch holds up with real depth behind it now, not just one card per path. Judgment Incarnate (Rare, Balance-keeper) is worth calling out specifically: it's a *new* payoff shape not seen in Volley or Storm — it inversely rewards staying at exactly 0 (max damage at Scale=0, falling off toward either extreme), which is a genuinely different scaling logic than Reaper/Ascendant's "more extreme = more reward." Balance-keeper isn't just "the path that ignores the mechanic" the way Artemis's Huntress or Thor's Unrelenting were — it has its own positive payoff shape.
- Scale-reading cards use three distinct query types: magnitude comparison (`\|Scale\| ≥ 3`), sign check (`Scale is negative`), and range/balance check (`between -1 and +1`). Worth flagging as an implementation note, not just a design one: the underlying resource-reading system needs to support all three query shapes generically, which is meaningfully more complex than Volley/Storm's simple "how much do I have" reads. Relevant whenever this moves toward actual Unity implementation.
- Osiris's Rebirth (Rare, Ascendant) is a safety-net effect, same category as Thor's Deathless Rage — flagging again rather than deciding: might belong as a relic instead of a card, worth a consistent ruling across all Blessings once one gets decided.
- Checked explicitly for the kind of accidental duplicate found in Thor's draft: Book of the Dead (Common) and Duat's Gaze (Uncommon) look similar at a glance but are deliberately tiered (Duat's Gaze adds a draw), not a duplicate — same pattern as Twin Strike/Twin Judgment. No unintentional duplicates found this pass.

**Not attempted**: numeric tuning, same caveat as Artemis and Thor.
