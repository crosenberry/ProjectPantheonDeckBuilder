# Artemis — Relics (Greek: Offense/Mobility)

First relic pool drafted for any Blessing — a curated slice proving the shape (per [GameDesignDocument.md §4](../GameDesignDocument.md): no rarity tiers, mythology-flavored, Greek = Offense/Mobility), not the complete eventual roster. Numbers are placeholders, same standing rule as everywhere else in this project (per [DifferentiationHooks.md](../DifferentiationHooks.md), tuning is deferred until playtesting).

**Silver Quiver** is not new — it was already established as a real Artemis relic in the [Syncretism prototype](../Syncretism/Artemis-Thor-Prototype.md) (its Shape 1/2 examples assume it exists); this is the first time it's part of a formal relic list rather than a standalone fusion example.

## Regular pool (6)

| Name | Flavor | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Silver Quiver | Offense | Your first Shot card each turn deals 3 additional damage. |
| Orion's Belt | Mobility | Your first card played each turn costs 1 less Energy (minimum 1). |
| Moonlit Bowstring | Mobility | At the start of each combat, gain 1 Volley. |
| Huntress's Snare | Offense | Whenever you apply Exposed, apply 1 additional stack. |
| Nymph's Ward | Mobility | The first time you would take damage each combat, gain 5 Block first. |
| Calydon's Mark | Offense | Whenever an enemy dies, deal 2 damage to a random other enemy. |

Calydon's Mark (the Calydonian Boar hunt, one of Artemis's clearest mythological ties) is the one relic here that specifically pays off multi-enemy fights (Viper Brood-style packs) rather than single-target duels — deliberate, since Artemis's base 25-card slice and passive tree are both otherwise fairly single-target-heavy.

## Boss-exclusive (1)

| Name | Effect |
|---|---|
| Bow of the Far-Shooter | Your Shot cards deal 2 additional damage to enemies below 50% HP. |

"Far-Shooter" (*hekēbolos*) is a real Homeric epithet shared by Apollo and Artemis. Deliberately **not** a safety-net effect (survive-lethal-once) — that pattern is already ruled to stay card-only, not relic-only, per [CrossBlessing-Comparison.md](CrossBlessing-Comparison.md)'s "safety net" finding ("a relic version would be an always-on insurance policy with no real opportunity cost"). Also deliberately does **not** touch Volley's per-turn reset rule — that exception is reserved specifically for the passive tree's Mythic capstones (Practiced Patience), per [GameDesignDocument.md §8](../GameDesignDocument.md); a relic bending the same rule would cheapen that being a Mythic-only privilege.

## Sanity checks

**Count**: 6 regular + 1 boss-exclusive, resolving the [GameDesignDocument.md §11](../GameDesignDocument.md) open question of "relic count per mythology + boss-exclusive relic count" — this count is the starting slice for Greek specifically, not a claimed final number for all 4 mythologies (each mythology may reasonably want a different count later; not decided here).

**No rule violations**: nothing here changes reward-pool odds, nothing bends Volley's reset outside the already-established Mythic-capstone exception, nothing duplicates the safety-net-stays-a-card ruling.

**Not attempted**: numeric tuning (all values placeholder), and the other 3 mythologies' relic pools (out of scope for M3, which is Artemis-only).
